How to use Teto Relay

Speak or sing into your microphone and Teto sings it back, in your rhythm and your melody.

Before you start First start Discord & OBS The controls Tips Troubleshooting Your files

Before you start

Teto Relay doesn't include these. Install each one once; Check setup in the app tells you if one is missing.

Needed

.NET 8 Desktop Runtime

Runs OpenUtau's singing engine. Get ".NET Desktop Runtime 8" for Windows x64.

Needed

OpenUtau

Its engine does the singing. You never need to open it. Download OpenUtau.

Needed

A UTAU voicebank

Her voice: a folder with an oto.ini and .wav files. Kasane Teto's "tandoku" bank works best; other UTAU banks work too.

For Discord / OBS

VB-Cable

A virtual cable Teto sings into, so other apps can use her as a microphone. Get VB-Cable, then restart Windows.

First start

About five minutes, once. After that it's just steps 5 and 6.

  1. Open Teto Relay

    The control panel opens in its own window. To keep it handy, right-click its taskbar button and choose Pin to taskbar. Closing the window closes Teto Relay.

  2. Press Check setup

    It's below the settings. Fix every line marked [FAIL] first; it says how. [WARN] lines are optional.

  3. Add a voicebank

    Drop a voicebank's .zip onto Add a voicebank, or point All settings → Setup → Voicebank folder at the folder you keep them in. Pick one from the list beside her picture. You can rename it there too.

  4. Pick your microphone and output

    Set Microphone to your real mic and Output to CABLE Input (or your speakers, to hear her yourself). Settings apply as soon as you change them.

  5. Press Start

    The first start may download a speech model, so give it a minute or two. Later starts take seconds. A loading bar shows how far along it is.

  6. Hold F8, speak, let go

    Teto sings it a couple of seconds later. Hold the key for the whole phrase, even through pauses: there's no time limit.

Hear her in Discord, OBS or a game

Teto sings into VB-Cable; the other app listens to the cable as if it were a microphone.

Your mic→Teto Relay→CABLE Input→CABLE Output→Discord / OBS

In Discord: User Settings → Voice & Video → Input Device → CABLE Output. In OBS: add an Audio Input Capture source set to CABLE Output.

Never set Teto Relay's Microphone to CABLE Output. She would hear herself and loop.

The controls

Everything on the main screen. The rest is under All settings, and every setting explains itself.

Language

The language you speak: English, ไทย or 日本語. Any voicebank sings any of them: a Japanese bank sings English or Thai as Japanese syllables.

Singing style

Speech follows your voice exactly. Sung puts it in a key, with glides between notes and vibrato on long ones.

Double voice

A fuller, layered sound on the phrases you sing. Phrases you speak stay single.

Song lyrics

Singing a song? Paste the lines first and every word is heard right. Clear it when you go back to talking.

Transpose & Volume

Move her whole range up or down in semitones, and set how loud she is.

Push-to-talk key

F8 by default. Change it if another app uses that key. Under Recording you can switch to hands-free.

The two buttons at the top right switch light/dark mode and turn animations off on a slow PC.

Tips for the best result

  • Speak or sing clearly, like you mean it. She copies your rhythm and pitch, so an expressive voice gives an expressive Teto.
  • A phrase at a time. She sings when you let go of the key, so shorter phrases come back sooner.
  • Leave a pause where you want a rest. Words said together are sung joined up.
  • A word comes out wrong? Turn on All settings → Setup → Keep what I said. Each phrase you say is saved next to what she sang, so the problem can be checked.
  • Slow? With an NVIDIA graphics card, set Listen on to cuda.

Troubleshooting

Start with Check setup: it lists anything missing, with the fix.

What you seeWhat to do
Plain beeps instead of TetoOpenUtau couldn't start. Usually the .NET 8 Desktop Runtime is missing or the OpenUtau folder is wrong; Check setup names the reason.
"No output device matching CABLE Input"Install VB-Cable and restart Windows, or choose another Output.
"The microphone is not available"Plug it in, or close any app using it exclusively. Teto Relay keeps retrying; if it doesn't come back, press Stop and Start.
She hears herself and loopsYour Microphone is set to CABLE Output. Set it to your real mic.
Nothing happens when you hold F8Another app may be taking the key. Change Push-to-talk key.
A word is silent or sung oddlyIt may not be in the dictionary. Turn on Keep what I said and report it with the saved files.
